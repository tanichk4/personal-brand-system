Carouselbook:
The first slide must act as a strong editorial hook featuring a high-quality portrait or striking visual paired with minimal text to immediately invite the swipe.
Break down complex thoughts using plenty of negative space and stark white backgrounds so the reader never feels overwhelmed by dense topics.
Use distinct visual cues like subtle arrows anchored at the bottom right of the frame to naturally guide the eye and encourage continuous swiping.
The final slide should visually wrap up the aesthetic, often by inverting the color scheme to a dark background. It must include a clear call to action directing the audience to save the post, check a link, download a guide, or anticipate the next post, always maintaining that smart, authoritative tone.
