Overall vibe: Smart, confident, feminine, calm, and airy. The look is a high-end editorial blend of crisp business authority and polished lifestyle aesthetics. Never come across as desperate, lost, or untidy.
Fonts: The main font is Inter for clean, highly legible body copy. The secondary font is Playfair Display for high-impact, elegant headers. There should be no all-caps, no underlines, and no emojis. Everything sits straight with no tilted or rotated text.
Visuals and Effects: Apply soft blurs and smooth gradients to background photography to push busy elements out of focus so overlaid text remains highly legible. Place soft drop shadows behind text, cutout portraits, models, and diagrams to create subtle depth and make elements pop off the flat backgrounds without harsh lines. Use soft dissolve and highlight effects to gently draw attention to specific words or visual transitions, keeping the composition deliberate.
Colors and Layouts: Color grading leans into high-contrast neutrals like stark white and deep charcoal, with occasional strategic use of a single vibrant accent color to draw the eye to key concepts. Photographic layouts utilize full-bleed images, clean cutouts on solid backgrounds, and horizontal split screens to pack multiple scenes into a single frame.
Carouselbook:
The first slide must act as a strong editorial hook featuring a high-quality portrait or striking visual paired with minimal text to immediately invite the swipe.
Break down complex thoughts using plenty of negative space and stark white backgrounds so the reader never feels overwhelmed by dense topics.
Use distinct visual cues like subtle arrows anchored at the bottom right of the frame to naturally guide the eye and encourage continuous swiping.
The final slide should visually wrap up the aesthetic, often by inverting the color scheme to a dark background. It must include a clear call to action directing the audience to save the post, check a link, download a guide, or anticipate the next post, always maintaining that smart, authoritative tone.
