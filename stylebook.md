Overall vibe: Smart, confident, feminine, calm, and airy. The look is a high-end editorial blend of crisp business authority and polished lifestyle aesthetics. Never come across as desperate, lost, or untidy.
Fonts: Inter + EB Garamond Italic. Both are free on Google Fonts (OFL, commercial use OK) and support Latin and full Ukrainian Cyrillic (Ґ Є І Ї).

| Role | Font |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | Inter 600 |
| Accent word | EB Garamond Italic 400 |
| Body | Inter 400 |

- One accent word per slide or frame: the keyword, set in EB Garamond Italic at about 118% of the text around it, same colour as the text.
- No other fonts. Not used: serif headlines, Playfair Display, Cormorant, Unbounded/Bounded, Montserrat, Lora, Manrope.
- There should be no all-caps, no underlines, and no emojis. Everything sits straight with no tilted or rotated text.

Visuals and Effects: Apply soft blurs and smooth gradients to background photography to push busy elements out of focus so overlaid text remains highly legible. Place soft drop shadows behind text, cutout portraits, models, and diagrams to create subtle depth and make elements pop off the flat backgrounds without harsh lines. Use soft dissolve and highlight effects to gently draw attention to specific words or visual transitions, keeping the composition deliberate.
Colors and Layouts: Color grading leans into high-contrast neutrals like stark white and deep charcoal, with occasional strategic use of a single vibrant accent color to draw the eye to key concepts.

Accent colour: one per carousel or post, chosen each time (Tetiana names the main colour, or it follows the topic, e.g. green for nature, blue for career, red for love). It is used as a semi-transparent marker behind the accent word (the colour at about 40% on white, 55% on dark), like a highlighter stroke. The text itself is never coloured. Default when nothing fits: soft pink #F2A7C3. Photographic layouts utilize full-bleed images, clean cutouts on solid backgrounds, and horizontal split screens to pack multiple scenes into a single frame.
